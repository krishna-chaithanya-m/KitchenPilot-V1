## 1. Executive Summary

This report presents a comprehensive, read-only scientific and data-quality audit of the nutrition outputs generated by the KitchenPilot-V1 Nutrition Engine. The audit independently verifies mathematical aggregation precision, evaluates population-level nutrient distributions, analyzes catastrophic outliers, audits missing caloric contributors, and categorizes data quality defects across the 6,871 recipes and 84,246 ingredient rows in the corpus.

### Key Audit Findings:

1. **Mathematical Engine Soundness (100% Pass):**
   - All 28 ingredient spot checks covering 14 operational scenarios (metric mass, volume conversions, unitless counts, fractions, proxy mappings, qualitative quantities, and state mismatches) perfectly matched independent calculations against raw CNF per-100g nutrient profiles (`calculated = cnf_per_100g * grams / 100`).
   - All 25 sampled recipe aggregations passed 100% without arithmetic discrepancy (`recipe_total == sum(ingredient_totals)` and `per_serving == total / servings`).
   - Zero negative nutrient values exist across all 6,871 recipes and 14 monitored macro/micronutrient dimensions.
   - Calorie consistency checks against macro-derived Atwater factors (4P + 4C + 9F) demonstrated high fidelity (typical variance within 2% to 6%), consistent with dietary fiber deductions and CNF specific energy factors.

2. **Source Data Formatting & Extreme Metric Outliers (CRITICAL):**
   - Catastrophic per-serving calorie outliers were identified (peaking at 188,354 kcal/serving and 178,638 kcal/serving).
   - Root-cause analysis traced these anomalies directly to upstream text entry errors in raw recipe files where kilograms were recorded instead of grams (e.g. `750 Kg Chicken` in `R05587`, yielding 750,000g / 892,500 kcal) and liters instead of milliliters (e.g. `200 liter Coconut milk` in `R07106`, yielding 191,040g / 376,348 kcal).
   - Unitless measure mapping errors in whole nuts (e.g., `30 Badam (Almond)` in `R08905` mapped to CNF whole item measure `'100 ml whole'` weighing 60.02g each instead of ~1.2g/nut, yielding 1,800.5g / 10,424 kcal).

3. **Extensive Caloric Undercounting in Major Ingredients (HIGH):**
   - **8,108 out of 15,742 major caloric ingredient rows (51.50%)** contribute **0 calories** to their respective recipes.
   - **4,587 recipes (66.76% of the corpus)** suffer from at least one major caloric ingredient (oil, ghee, butter, sugar, rice, flour, dal, nuts, paneer, milk) being zeroed out.
   - Primary causes include unmapped ingredients (3,255 rows), conversion failures (1,287 rows), missing quantities (1,123 rows), NO_MATCH curated status (1,117 rows), qualitative quantities (1,056 rows), and state mismatches (255 rows).

4. **Coverage and Quality Status Realities:**
   - Currently, **99.65% of recipes (6,847)** are graded `PARTIAL`, **0.28% (19)** are `COMPLETE`, and **0.07% (5)** are `APPROXIMATE`.
   - Critical staple Indian foods lack CNF mappings (`NO_MATCH`), notably `urad dal` (718 recipes), `chana dal` (606 recipes), `curd / yogurt` (1,288 recipes), `tamarind` (611 recipes), `cashew` (533 recipes), `jaggery` (398 recipes), and `paneer` (304 recipes).
   - 289 recipes (4.21%) produce **0 total calories** due to unmapped ingredients, qualitative quantities, or missing ingredient records in the raw dataset.

1. **Extreme Right Skewness:**
   - While the median `per_serving_calories_kcal` is **73.8 kcal** and the 75th percentile is **172.1 kcal**, the mean is elevated to **192.7 kcal** and the maximum reaches **188,354.5 kcal**.
   - The standard deviation of per-serving calories (**3,137.1 kcal**) exceeds the mean by an order of magnitude, clearly signaling extreme outlier contamination rather than Gaussian variation.
2. **Under-estimation of Lower Quartiles:**
   - At P25, `per_serving_calories_kcal` is only **26.1 kcal**, and median protein is only **1.51 g**.
   - This depressed median reflects systemic undercounting from unmapped staple ingredients (urad dal, paneer, wheat flour) and zeroed qualitative cooking oils rather than truly low-calorie recipes.

   - Median per-serving sodium is **19.1 mg**, with P95 reaching **485.2 mg**.

## 15. Findings Classified by Severity

### CRITICAL
1. **Unit Prefix Confusion in Raw Data Sources:**
   - Source text containing `750 Kg Chicken` (`R05587`) and `200 liter Coconut milk` (`R07106`) where grams and milliliters were intended. The engine faithfully executed metric multipliers, generating impossible per-serving values (> 170,000 kcal).
2. **Volumetric Container Measure Mapped to Whole Nut Count:**
   - Unitless quantities for whole almonds (e.g. `30 Badam (Almond)` in `R08905`) were mapped to the CNF measure `'100 ml whole'` (60.017g each) rather than an individual nut mass (~1.2g), inflating nut mass 50x and causing 10,000+ kcal outliers.
3. **Staple Flour Unmapped Defect:**
   - `Whole Wheat Flour` / `Atta` lacks canonical mapping across numerous staple bread recipes (e.g. `R00055 Rajasthani very roti`, `R01198 Bajri Roti`), reducing primary breads to 0 calories.

### HIGH
1. **Pervasive Caloric Undercounting in Major Ingredients:**
   - 8,108 out of 15,742 major caloric ingredient rows (51.5%) produced 0 calories, affecting 4,587 recipes (66.76% of corpus). Cooking fats (1,979 oil rows, 1,098 ghee rows) and staple flours (1,684 flour rows) are heavily suppressed.
2. **Core Indian Protein & Staple Foods Marked `NO_MATCH`:**
   - Inability to match staple Indian pulses and dairy in CNF (`urad dal` in 718 recipes, `chana dal` in 606 recipes, `curd` in 1,288 recipes, `paneer` in 304 recipes, `cashews` in 533 recipes, `jaggery` in 398 recipes) systematically deflates protein and energy metrics across traditional Indian meal profiles.

### MEDIUM
1. **Qualitative Quantities for Fats and Flours Zeroed Out:**
   - 6,453 qualitative rows across 4,590 recipes produce 0 calories. While appropriate for salt, zeroing `oil as required` (1,025 major fat rows) depresses fat intake by an estimated 10–25g per recipe.
2. **State Mismatch Zeroing Out Cooked Staples:**
   - 820 rows across 734 recipes are completely zeroed out due to `STATE_MISMATCH` because preparation text specified cooked/boiled while CNF reference was raw/dry (e.g. `2-1/2 cups rice - cooked`).
3. **Extensive Unmapped Ingredient Corpus:**
   - 19,530 ingredient rows (23.18% of all rows) remain unmapped across 6,222 recipes.

### LOW
1. **Missing Ingredient Text in Raw Source Corpus:**
   - 6 recipes (`R00306`, `R01413`, `R02072`, `R02098`, `R07894`, `R08388`) have empty ingredient strings in `recipes.csv`, causing 0-ingredient entries.
2. **Proxy Variance in `NEEDS_REVIEW`:**
   - 9 proxy mappings affecting 4,262 rows introduce minor (< 1%) micronutrient approximation, though macro contributions remain sound.
3. **Unresolved Non-Standard Physical Units:**
   - Rare or ambiguous culinary units (`sprig`, `inch`, `clove` for non-garlic items) failing mass conversion.

### OBSERVATION
1. **100% Mathematical & Conservation Precision:**
   - The nutrition aggregation engine is mathematically robust, deterministic, and preserves exact mass and energy conservation across all verified spot checks.
2. **Conservative Quality Stratification Working as Designed:**
   - The engine correctly assigned 99.65% of recipes to `PARTIAL` because of existing data-quality and mapping gaps, preventing unverified or incomplete estimates from being falsely certified as `COMPLETE`.

## 16. Recommended Fixes for the Next Engineering Stage

To elevate the nutrition engine from baseline execution to production-grade recommendation inputs, the following engineering enhancements are recommended in prioritized sequence:

1. **Unit Sanity Validation & Plausibility Clamping:**
   - Implement an automated sanity filter during parsing: if a single recipe ingredient exceeds realistic culinary thresholds (e.g. `> 10 kg` meat or `> 10 liters` liquid for standard household servings $\le 10$), flag and re-evaluate unit prefix (e.g. interpret `750 Kg Chicken` as 750 g; `200 liter Coconut milk` as 200 ml).
   - Add per-serving calorie outlier clamping/flagging for recipes exceeding 5,000 kcal/serving.

2. **Curated Nut & Whole Item Measure Calibration:**
   - Fix unitless count conversions for nuts: map unitless almonds, cashews, and walnuts to an individual nut unit mass (~1.2g for almond, ~1.5g for cashew) rather than CNF volumetric container measures (`'100 ml whole'`).

3. **Expand Indian Food Composition References (Resolve `NO_MATCH`):**
   - For items absent in the Canadian Nutrient File (`urad dal`, `chana dal`, `paneer`, `curd`, `tamarind`, `jaggery`), integrate authoritative nutritional profiles from the Indian Food Composition Tables (IFCT / ICMR-NIN) or curated USDA equivalents.

4. **Resolve State Mismatches with Moisture Yield Factors:**
   - Instead of zeroing `STATE_MISMATCH` ingredients, implement standard cooking yield factors: convert cooked rice to raw rice equivalent ($1\text{g cooked} \approx 0.4\text{g raw}$) or match directly to cooked CNF rice/dal entries.

5. **Default Imputation for Qualitative Cooking Fats:**
   - For `oil as required` or `ghee for frying`, introduce a conservative culinary baseline (e.g. 1 tablespoon / 14g oil per 4 servings) tagged under an `ESTIMATED_QUALITATIVE` provenance flag rather than zeroing out all fat.

6. **Canonical Mapping for Staple Flours:**
   - Ensure `Whole Wheat Flour`, `Atta`, `Maida`, `Besan`, and `Rice Flour` are explicitly linked to curated CNF cereal entries.
